What we do

We bring farm products such as ghee, cold-pressed oils, dry fruits and nuts straight to your home. You order online, and we deliver to your door in Bengaluru on weekends.

Why we do it

  • To give families products they can trace back to the source.
  • To cut out middlemen, so the farm gets a fair share.
  • To make buying good food simple: order online, pay by UPI.
